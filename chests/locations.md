# Chests' locations

There's a guide providing the location of 87 chests
([url](https://github.com/RetroAchievements/guides/wiki/Tomba!-(PlayStation)#treasure-collector-missable)).

Those six additional chests are not listed in it :

| N° | Location         | Type    | Contents         |
|----|------------------|---------|------------------|
| 88 | Phoenix Mountain | 100     | Charity Wings x2 |
| 89 | Phoenix Mountain | 1,000   | Large Lunch Box  |
| 90 | Phoenix Mountain | Million | Iron Boomerang   |
| 91 | Haunted Mansion  | 100     | Cheese x2        |
| 92 | Haunted Mansion  | 1,000   | Lunch Box        |
| 93 | Haunted Mansion  | 1,000   | Boss' Jewel      |

Note : VGCartography on [DeviantArt](https://www.deviantart.com/vgcartography/gallery/79541945/tomba-1997) was a nice
resource too to be sure that no other chest was missed.

The chests will be described in the order of the aforementioned guide, then followed by the six others above.
